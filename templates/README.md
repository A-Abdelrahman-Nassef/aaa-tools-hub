# PowerPoint template for the Areas annex

The Areas module builds "Annex D – Areas Calculation" on the first .pptx/.potx here (by name).

`AAA Annex template.pptx` is the delivered Sample Villa Annex D deck, used as the AAA template until the official one
arrives. When a template has slides, they give the brand:

- **Slide 1 = cover.** Its texts are replaced: "ANNEX D" (the annex letter), the title ("AREAS CALCULATION"), and the
  CLIENT: / PROJECT: / TITLE: / DATE: lines. Logo, background and chevrons stay as they are.
- **Slide 2 = page frame**, copied for every content slide: header bar, AAA CONSULTANCY, section label, title and the
  "PAGE |" footer. Whatever sits between the title and the footer is dropped.
- **Last slide = back cover.** Its longest text becomes the closing note; the logos stay.
- Every other slide is dropped.

A template without slides is used through its layouts instead (cover: "Title Slide"/"Cover"; content: "Title Only",
else "Title and Content"). With no file here the annex uses a plain AAA navy layout.

To switch to the official AAA template, replace the file here (keep one file, or make sure it sorts first).
